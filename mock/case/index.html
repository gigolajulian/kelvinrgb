What this shows. The Live strips panel becomes a side view of your case. Every LED is a dot at its place, lit by the same frame the PC gets.
Across the case runs one effect over the whole layout, so a Sweep enters the RAM, crosses the cooler and leaves through the front fans. Each device is today's behaviour.
Direction picks the axis. Arrange lets you drag each device to where it really sits; KelvinRGB starts from a guess by device type. The mouse sits on the desk at the right.
Try: switch effects and modes, change the direction, press Arrange and drag the GPU or the RAM, then press Done.
